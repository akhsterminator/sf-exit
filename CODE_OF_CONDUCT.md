# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment or personal attacks of any kind are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/akhsterminator/sf-exit/security/advisories/new),
or by opening an issue that asks a maintainer to contact you. Reports are handled confidentially.
